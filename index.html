About Chris

A local agent who knows what homes here are really worth.

"If you're looking for an agent who listens and understands your specific needs, let's talk."

I'm a Real Estate Broker with Coldwell Banker Realty in Lake Forest, Illinois, and I've proudly served clients since 2003, all from the same trusted office. As a member of the MercFoss Team, I specialize in high-end residential real estate and bring commercial real estate experience to every deal.

My focus is local property knowledge and valuation. I help clients understand exactly where the market is, so they can time and position their listings and offers strategically.

I was raised in Lake Forest and now live in the Whispering Oaks neighborhood, so I bring not just professional knowledge but a real feel for the local lifestyle and community.

Whether you're buying your first home, moving up, downsizing, or exploring an investment, I'll guide you every step of the way.
